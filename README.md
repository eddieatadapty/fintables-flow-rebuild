# Fintables Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/fintables-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Fintables users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Fintables's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Lead with one plan: the one that fits what they invest in | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | A quick win on their own stocks before the price | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Say what a year costs per month | +10–15% ARPU (more yearly plans) |
| 04 | Show your 4.7 stars before the first question | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +5–10% CR to purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Fintables's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Fintables. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
